How to use this — 60 second version

  1. Type your name on the first screen and hit START CALLING.
  2. The screen shows you ONE person. Nobody else on your team gets this person at the same time, so you can't double-call anyone.
  3. Hit the big green CALL NOW button (or dial the number shown).
  4. Read the middle panel out loud. It's written word-for-word. You do not need to invent anything.
  5. When the call ends, tap one of the coloured buttons on the right that matches what happened.
  6. Hit SAVE & NEXT LEAD. A new person appears. Repeat.

What the buttons mean

Rules that keep us out of trouble

  • Call between 11:00 and 20:00 only. Never before 10am, never after 9pm.
  • If they say "don't call me again" — tap Do Not Call Again. Never dial them again. This matters legally.
  • Never argue. Never lie about a property. Never promise a price.
  • If they're angry, say: "Bilkul, main abhi aapka number list se nikaal deta hoon. Maazrat." Then tap Do Not Call Again.
  • The system retries No Answer automatically. Don't redial manually 10 times.

Where's my data?

Everything saves automatically to the server the second you tap SAVE. The manager can download the whole thing as an Excel file from the Dashboard tab at any time.
